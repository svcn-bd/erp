How to pay your bill

  1. Log in with your customer ID or registered mobile number.
  2. Open the payment section and send the amount by bKash, following the steps shown there.
  3. Paste your transaction ID and submit it.
  4. We verify it, and your account is recharged. Your receipt appears in the portal.
